Key Title URL

Keys are lowercase letters and digits. No key may be the start of another one — that is what lets a completed key fire the moment it is typed. Rows with an error are kept on screen but left out of the saved set. A URL without a scheme gets https:// put in front of it. Saved to browser sync storage.

Import

Paste a list of shortcuts — this page's own format, or a ShortcutKey2URL export. Keys are lowercased, a key that already exists is replaced, and entries without a URL are skipped.
